Stampede: Squad Roguelite
Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0 and later · Developer: CoffeAddiction

In short

Stampede does not ask you to create an account and keeps your game progress only on your device; we (the developer) do not collect, see or store any personal data. The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve those ads, Google collects the device and usage data described below. This version contains no in-app purchases.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (level, coins and gems, heroes, weapons, quests), your settings and your chosen language are stored only on your own device. This information is not sent to us and is not shared with anyone.

You can delete it at any time: use Settings → Reset progress in the game, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game has two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded videos: entirely optional. They only play when you tap a “watch” button for a reward (energy, a crate, doubled coins, a revive and so on).
  • Interstitials: full-screen ads shown between levels, only after a certain level and at a limited frequency.

Ads are served by Google LLC's AdMob service. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google's ad software automatically collects the following data and sends it to Google:

  • IP address (which may be used to estimate the device's general location),
  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID,
  • App and ad interactions: app launches, taps, ad views and clicks,
  • Diagnostic information: the performance of the app and the ad software (launch time, hang rate and similar), device model, operating system version and language.

This data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Google processes it under its own privacy policy; see the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ad content is limited to the “Teen” rating at most.

Consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form on first launch, where you decide whether to allow personalised ads. You can change your choice later under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded videos are always optional; you can play the game without watching any of them.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads. The game itself also plays offline.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by Google's ad software for the purposes described above.
  • Vibration: used only for in-game haptic feedback; it can be turned off in settings.

The game does not access your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Purchases

This version contains no in-app purchases. If they are added later, payments will go through Google Play Billing; we never see your payment details, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Deleting data

We hold no records about you. You can delete the game data on your device as described above. For ad data, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and manage data tied to your Google Account at Google Account.

Google Play

When you download the game from Google Play, the download and updates are handled by Google and Google's Privacy Policy applies.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the current version on this page and update the effective date.
