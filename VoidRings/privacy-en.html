Void Rings
Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026 · Developer: Yusuf Mutlu (CaffeAddiction)

In short

Void Rings does not ask you to create an account, and your game progress is stored only on your device; we (the developer) do not collect, see or store any personal data. The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve these ads, Google collects the device and usage data described below. There are no in-app purchases; the crystals in the game are earned only by playing and are never sold for real money.

What is stored on your device

Your best score, your crystals, the galaxies you unlocked and the galaxy you selected, the ships, colors and light trails you bought and equipped, the workshop parts you bought and the ship you designed, your shop upgrades, your game statistics (such as games played, gates passed and crystals collected), your daily reward streak, daily missions and achievements, and your sound, music and graphics quality settings are stored only on your own device. This information is never sent to us or shared with anyone.

You can delete this data at any time: use the İlerlemeyi sıfırla (Reset progress) button on the game's Settings (Ayarlar) screen, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. If device backup is turned on in Android, this data may be included in the backup in your Google Account; we have no access to that backup.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game has two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded ads: fully optional; shown only if you choose to watch. They are offered in three places: a “continue” offer for a few seconds when your shields run out (at most once per game), at the end of a game to double the crystals you earned in that game, and on the daily reward screen to double the reward.
  • Interstitial ads: shown once every 3 games, after a game ends, with at least 90 seconds between two interstitial ads. They are not shown if you watched a rewarded ad in that game.

There are no banner ads, and no ad ever interrupts gameplay.

Ads are served by Google LLC's AdMob service. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google's ad software automatically collects the following data and sends it to Google:

  • IP address (may be used to estimate the device's approximate location),
  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID,
  • App and ad interactions: ad views and taps,
  • Diagnostics: performance and errors of the ad software, device model, operating system version and language.

This data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Google processes it under its own privacy policy; see the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ad content is limited to the “Parental Guidance” rating at most.

Consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent screen on first launch, and you decide whether to allow personalized ads. You can change your choice later in the game under Ayarlar → Reklam gizliliği (Settings → Ad privacy).
  • Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalized ads in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional; you can play the whole game without watching any rewarded ads.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only to load ads. The game itself can be played offline.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) and Android ad services: used by Google's ad software for the purposes described above.

The game does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Deleting data

We hold no records about you. You can delete the game data on your device as described above. For ad data, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and manage data tied to your Google Account at Google Account.

Google Play

When you download the game from Google Play, the download and updates are handled by Google and Google's Privacy Policy applies.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the current version on this page and update the effective date.
