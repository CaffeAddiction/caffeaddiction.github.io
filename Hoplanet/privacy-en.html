Hoplanet
Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Developer: Yusuf Mutlu (CaffeAddiction)

In short

Hoplanet does not ask you to create an account, and your game progress is stored only on your device; we (the developer) do not collect, see or store any personal data. The game is free and shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve these ads, Google collects the device and usage data described below. There are no in-app purchases.

What is stored on your device

Your best score, your stars, your progress on each map and the stations you reached, your best endless-run score and the farthest map you reached, the game mode you chose, the balls and trails you bought and equipped, the daily goal status, your sound and music settings and whether you finished the tutorial are stored only on your own device. This information is never sent to us or shared with anyone.

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. If device backup is turned on in Android, this data may be included in the backup in your Google Account; we have no access to that backup.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game has only one kind of ad:

  • Rewarded ads: fully optional. When you lose a run, a “Watch and continue” button appears for a few seconds; if you choose to watch the ad, you continue from the planet where you were. It is offered at most once per section between two stations.

There are no interstitial or banner ads, and no ad ever interrupts gameplay.

Ads are served by Google LLC's AdMob service. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google's ad software automatically collects the following data and sends it to Google:

  • IP address (may be used to estimate the device's approximate location),
  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID,
  • App and ad interactions: ad views and taps,
  • Diagnostics: performance and errors of the ad software, device model, operating system version and language.

This data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Google processes it under its own privacy policy; see the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ad content is limited to the “Parental Guidance” rating at most.

Consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent screen on first launch, and you decide whether to allow personalized ads. You can change your choice later with the Privacy settings link at the top right of the home screen.
  • Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalized ads in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional; you can play the whole game without watching any ads.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only to load ads. The game itself can be played offline.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by Google's ad software for the purposes described above.
  • Vibration: only for short in-game haptic feedback.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Deleting data

We hold no records about you. You can delete the game data on your device as described above. For ad data, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and manage data tied to your Google Account at Google Account.

Google Play

When you download the game from Google Play, the download and updates are handled by Google and Google's Privacy Policy applies.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the current version on this page and update the effective date.
