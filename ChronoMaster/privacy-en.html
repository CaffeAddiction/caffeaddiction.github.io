Privacy Policy

ChronoMaster: Master Halil's Clock Workshop · Effective date: 10 October 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains which data is processed while you play the ChronoMaster mobile game (the “Game”). The Game is developed by Yusuf Mutlu (“we”). This is a translation of the Turkish policy; if the two differ, the Turkish text applies.

In short

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, e-mail or location.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your own device and is not sent to us.
  • Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one for a reward, and are served by Google AdMob.

Data stored on your device

Game progress such as your level, stars, gears, workshop repairs, settings and daily reward streak is kept in your device's local storage. This data is not sent to our servers and cannot be seen by us. You can delete it at any time with Settings > “Reset progress” or by uninstalling the Game.

Settings > “Back up progress” produces a backup code so you can move your progress to another phone. The code contains only your game progress and nothing about your identity, and it goes only where you copy and send it; the Game never sends it anywhere by itself.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Game uses only rewarded ads: watching an ad is always your choice. Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect data such as the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions in order to show and measure ads and to prevent abuse. You can read how Google uses this data on Google's page about partner sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form before personalised ads. You can change your choice later in the Game under Settings > “Ad privacy preferences”. You can also turn off personalised ads by deleting your advertising ID in your device's Settings > Google > Ads.

Permissions

  • Internet: used only to load ads. The Game can be played offline.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback; it can be turned off in Settings.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights (KVKK and GDPR)

We do not process your personal data on servers of our own. You can exercise your rights regarding ad data with Google. For questions under Turkey's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data or under the GDPR, you can write to us.

Changes

If we update this policy, we publish the new version on this page with a new effective date.
