Missio: Gladius Ludus · Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains which data is processed when you play Missio: Gladius Ludus (the “Game”, package name com.caffeaddiction.missio) on Android. The Game is developed by Yusuf Mutlu under the name CaffeAddiction (“we”).

In short

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, e-mail address or location.
  • We collect, store and sell no personal data. The Game has no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your own device.
  • Optional rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob.
  • Fights with a friend (co-op) connect the two phones directly.
  • Reminders are notifications created on your phone; nothing about them is sent to us.

Your game save

Your progress (your school, gladiators, gold, buildings, the clocks of training, lessons and repairs) and your settings (sound, music, language and reminders) are kept in the app's private storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one, for example to speed up a wait. These ads are served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with the ad, in order to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other places where it is required, Google's consent form is shown before ads are personalised. If you do not consent, you see non-personalised ads or none. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

Google's policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Fights with a friend (co-op)

In a co-op fight the two phones connect to each other directly (WebRTC). To find each other they use a public introduction server (PeerJS) and Google's STUN servers; these servers can see the room code shown on your screen and the two phones' IP addresses while the connection is being set up. During the fight, only your friend's phone receives your chosen gladiator's game details (name, class, level, statistics and equipment), the stake in game gold and your fight controls. The connection is encrypted, and nothing from it is stored by us.

Reminders (notifications)

The Game can leave reminders with your phone, for example when a new round begins, a training session or a lesson ends, the estate is full, armour is mended, or your spies and the men on the road come home. It asks you first, and Android then asks for its own permission. The reminders are created and scheduled on your phone; no data leaves your device for them. You can turn them off at any time in the Game's settings window or in your phone's settings.

Purchases

The current version sells nothing for real money. If diamond packs are offered in a later version, they will be bought through Google Play: Google handles the payment, and your payment details never reach us.

Internet and permissions

  • Internet: used only for ads and co-op fights. Playing on your own works offline.
  • Notifications: for the reminders above, only if you allow them.
  • Advertising ID: added by the Google ads library, used as described above.

Children

The Game shows arena violence and is not directed at children; its target audience is adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to show you, correct or delete. Your save is under your control on your device. For data processed by Google for ads, use the consent form, your phone's ad settings and Google's privacy tools.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
