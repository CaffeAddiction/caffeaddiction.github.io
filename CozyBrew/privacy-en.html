Privacy Policy

Cozy Brew: Potion Shop · Effective date: 13 October 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains which data is processed while you play the Cozy Brew (Alchemist's Cozy Brew) mobile game (the “Game”). The Game is developed by Yusuf Mutlu (“we”). This is a translation of the Turkish policy; if the two differ, the Turkish text applies.

In short

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, e-mail or location.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your own device and is not sent to us.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Most are ads you choose to watch for a reward; now and then a full-screen ad may also appear when you return from a menu to the game.

Data stored on your device

Game progress such as your gold, gems, level, upgrades, decorations and daily quests, along with your language and sound settings, is kept in your device's local storage. This data is not sent to our servers and cannot be seen by us. You can delete it at any time with Settings > “Reset progress” or by uninstalling the Game.

If device backup is turned on in Android, app data may be included in the backup in your own Google account. We have no access to that backup.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Game has two kinds of ads: rewarded ads, which are always your choice to watch, and full-screen ads that are shown occasionally when you close a menu and return to the game. Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect data such as the advertising ID, IP address (from which approximate location is derived), device and app information and ad interactions in order to show and measure ads and to prevent abuse. You can read how Google uses this data on Google's page about partner sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form before personalised ads. You can change your choice later in the Game under Settings > “Ad privacy choices”. You can also turn off personalised ads by deleting your advertising ID in your device's Settings > Google > Ads.

Permissions

  • Internet: used only to load ads. The Game can be played offline.
  • Advertising ID: used by the advertising SDK to show and measure ads.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback; it can be turned off in Settings.

Data retention and deletion

Your game progress stays only on your device until you reset it or uninstall the Game. Data related to ads is retained by Google under its own policies; we do not hold it.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights (KVKK and GDPR)

We do not process your personal data on servers of our own. You can exercise your rights regarding ad data with Google. For questions under Turkey's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data or under the GDPR, you can write to us.

Changes

If we update this policy, we publish the new version on this page with a new effective date.
