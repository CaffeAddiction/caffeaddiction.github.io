Bloomsters: Seed Monsters
Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Developer: Yusuf Mutlu (CaffeAddiction) · Contact: mutluy082@gmail.com

This policy applies to the Bloomsters Android app (package name com.caffeaddiction.bloomsters).

In short

Bloomsters is designed for children and young teens (ages 0–15). It does not ask you to create an account, and your game progress is kept on your device. We (the developer) do not collect, see or store any personal data. The game shows only optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, and offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play. For players under 13, ads are child-directed and every purchase is behind a grown-up check.

Age screen

On first launch the game asks for your birth year. The year itself is not stored: only an age group (under 13, 13–17, or 18 and over) is kept on your device. It is used to choose suitable ad settings and child protections. The ad service is not started at all until the age screen has been completed.

What is stored on your device

Your garden, collection, coins and gems, settings (sound, music, vibration, language, text size), age group, and the receipts of completed purchases (so that a purchase is never delivered twice) are stored on your device. This information is not sent to us and is not shared with anyone.

Backup: if Android backup is turned on for your device, a copy of the game save (which contains no personal information) is backed up by Android's backup service to your own Google Account and restored when you move to a new device. That backup is stored by Google; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup.

You can delete your game data at any time: use Settings → Reset progress in the game, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows only rewarded ads. An ad plays only when you tap a button marked “Ad” to get an optional reward (for example, to speed up a plant or to double a daily gift), and each kind of reward has a daily limit. There are no banner ads, no interstitial ads and no ads when the app opens. Everything in the game can be done without watching ads. A grown-up can turn off all ad buttons in the Grown-ups section (see below).

Ad requests are configured by age group:

  • Under 13: requests are marked as child-directed (in line with COPPA and the Google Play Families Policy). Google does not use them for personalised advertising or remarketing, and ad content is limited to the “G” (general audiences) rating.
  • 13–17: requests are marked as coming from a user under the age of consent. No personalised ads are shown, and ad content is limited to the “PG” rating.
  • 18 and over: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown, where you decide whether to allow personalised ads. You can change your choice later with the Ad privacy options button in Settings. Ad content is limited to the “PG” rating.

Ads are served by Google LLC's AdMob service. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software may automatically collect and send to Google: the IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, subject to the age settings above), ad views and taps, and diagnostic information (performance and errors of the ad software, device model, operating system version and language). This data is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies: see the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation in Android settings.

In-app purchases

The game is free to play and offers optional purchases (gem packs, a starter pack and the Garden Pass). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play Billing; we never receive card or bank details. Only the product ID and the purchase token are processed on your device to deliver and restore the item. In child mode a grown-up check appears before every purchase, and a grown-up can turn purchases off entirely in the Grown-ups section.

Notifications

Notifications are off by default. If they are turned on (in child mode, only from the Grown-ups section), the game shows a local reminder on your device when your garden or lab is ready. Reminders are created on the device itself; no server, account or identifier is used to send them. At most 3 are shown per day, none between 21:00 and 08:00, and in child mode the game never sends time-limited reminders. You can turn them off in the game or in Android settings.

Analytics

The app contains a layer that can record in-game events (for example, finishing the tutorial) for players aged 13 and over. In this version these events are not sent to any server or analytics service. It is always off in child mode. If an analytics service is added later, this policy will be updated first.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load rewarded ads and to make purchases. The game itself plays offline.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by Google's ad software as described above, subject to the age settings.
  • Google Play Billing: for optional purchases.
  • Notifications: requested only if you turn reminders on.
  • Vibration: for in-game haptic feedback; it can be turned off in Settings.

The game does not access your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children and families

Bloomsters is designed for children and young teens and follows the Google Play Families Policy. The game does not ask for a name, email address, location, photos or any other personal information, and players cannot chat with or message each other.

In child mode (under 13):

  • a grown-up check appears before every purchase,
  • ads are child-directed and not personalised,
  • no usage analytics are collected,
  • a break reminder is on by default (every 30 minutes of continuous play).

The Grown-ups section in Settings (behind a grown-up check) lets a parent turn off rewarded ads entirely, turn off purchases, set the break reminder (off, 20, 30 or 45 minutes) and turn reminder notifications on or off.

Third-party services

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform — rewarded ads and consent (Google LLC)
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases (Google LLC)
  • Android Backup — backup of the game save to your Google Account (Google LLC)

Deleting data

We hold no records about you, so there is no account to delete. You can delete the game data on your device as described above. For ad data, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and manage data linked to your Google Account at myaccount.google.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the current version on this page and update the effective date.
