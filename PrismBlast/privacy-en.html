Privacy Policy

Prism Blast: Block Puzzle · Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains which data is processed while you play the Prism Blast mobile game (the “Game”, package name com.caffeaddiction.prismblast). The Game is developed by Yusuf Mutlu under the name CaffeAddiction (“we”). This is a translation of the Turkish policy; if the two differ, the Turkish text applies.

In short

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, e-mail or location.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your own device and is not sent to us.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob.
  • Purchases are optional and made through Google Play; your payment details never reach us.

Data stored on your device

Your level, scores, Spark balance, booster inventory, day and mastery streaks, daily puzzle result and settings (language, sound, haptics, colorblind mode, screen effects) are kept in your device's local storage. Purchase tokens issued by Google Play are also stored there so that the same purchase is never delivered twice. This data is not sent to our servers and cannot be seen by us.

The Game turns off Android backup, so this data is not backed up to your Google account. You can delete all of it with Settings > “Reset progress”, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the Game.

Sharing your results

When you tap “Share”, the Game creates an image showing your result (such as level, stars and score), writes it to your device's temporary cache and hands it only to the app you choose. The image contains nothing about your identity and is not sent to us. Sharing never starts on its own.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Game uses two kinds of ads: rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for a reward, and interstitial ads shown between rounds after level 4. Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect data such as the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics in order to show and measure ads and to prevent abuse. On supported devices it may also use Android's ad privacy APIs. You can read how Google uses this data on Google's page about partner sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent and your choices

  • Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form before personalised ads. You can change your choice later in the Game under Settings > “Ad privacy options”.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Watching a rewarded ad is always optional; skipping it never blocks your progress.
  • If you buy “Remove Ads”, interstitial ads are turned off permanently.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and purchases. The Game can be played offline.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback; it can be turned off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID: so that Google AdMob can show and measure ads.
  • Google Play billing: for optional purchases.

The ad SDK may declare additional permissions for Android's ad privacy APIs.

Purchases

In-game purchases (such as “Remove Ads” and the “Starter Pack”) are made with Google Play Billing. Your payment and card details are processed by Google and are not passed to us. To deliver and restore what you bought, we receive only the product ID, the purchase status and the purchase token from Google Play. You can restore your purchases in the Game under Store > “Restore purchases”.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Security

Connections for ads and purchases are encrypted (HTTPS); the Game does not allow unencrypted network traffic.

Deleting data and your rights (KVKK and GDPR)

We do not process your personal data on servers of our own. You can delete the data on your device at any time as described above. You can exercise your rights regarding ad and purchase data through your Google account. For questions under Turkey's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data or under the GDPR, you can write to us.

Google Play

The Game is distributed through Google Play. Google Play's own terms and privacy policy apply to downloads and purchases.

Changes

If we update this policy, we publish the new version on this page with a new effective date.
